## Customization

Two places to edit:

1. **`src/tensor_io.c`** — replace the placeholder `prepare_inputs()` and
   `consume_outputs()` with your app-specific data pipeline. The defaults
   write a deterministic byte pattern and print the first 8 output bytes.
2. **`config/service.json`** — adjust `restart_policy`, `keepalive_*`,
   and `license_features` to match your deployment requirements.
