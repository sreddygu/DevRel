**Input String:**  Connection - Connection Drop - Disconnection Triggered by STA - DHCP Failure

**Description**
1. DHCP failure even after 4way handshaking success

**Hostapd Log**
1. Look for lines that have the text 'Received Deauth with reason...'
2. Look for lines that have the text 'AP-STA-POSSIBLE-PSK-MISMATCH'

**Wifistats**
1. Look for m1_compl_fail, m3_compl_fail - These indicate failures in completing the M1 and M3 handshake messages, which are part of the 4-way WPA handshake.
2. Look for m1_packets, m1_success
3. Look for m3_packets, m3_success
4. Look for tx_mgmt_subtype_tx_comp_fail - Subtype 5 corresponds to Authentication frames. A high failure count here suggests issues during the authentication phase.
5. Look for tx_mgmt_subtype_tx_comp_fail - Subtype 13 is Deauthentication, which matches the STA-initiated deauth seen in the logs.
6. Look for xretry
7. Look for mpdu_tried_cnt, mpdu_queued_cnt
8. Look for fw_rx_mgmt_subtype - subtype 12 is Deauthentication received by the AP, confirming the STA is actively sending deauth frames.



**Input String:** Connection - Connection Failure - AP Scan Failure

**Description:**
1. No Probe response or No Beacon from AP

**Potential Root Causes:**
1. Beacon Stuck from AP
2. Beacon Tx power too low 
3. Drift from Beacon interval is beyond the allowed threshold (STA might go to sleep because of that)
4. AP fails to send out Probe Response (Very congested environment)
5. AP fails to receive Bcast/unicast probe request (Rx side issue)
6. Probe response flushed due to pending probe responses are high

**Critical Features and Logs for Issue Analysis:**
1. For beacon stuck, leverage beacon stuck type logging (FW stats3, beacon failure rate, and beacon interval)
2. Beacon tx power is not a concern. We have only rssi for RX pdev stats. For beacon tx power there are no stats.wifistats wifiX 10? 
3. Drift from beacon interval usually impact the clients that already associated not scan failure We can check the cnss diag's prints for TBTT counter to determine the same.	Line   9396: EXTMSG [0x013dc50b 69701400] SSID[4547] wlan_beacon_tx_offload.c Line[1224]: BEACON_DIAG: TX_FAIL pdev|vdev:0 seq_num:3867 TBTT:3620241408us compl_TS:3620241439us last_tx_ok_seq_num:3866 cons_fail:1 err_st:2 tx_attm:1 flush_rc:0
4. Tx/Rx Management frame failure rate (wifistats wifi2 21 --wmi )
5. Sniffer log from monitor vap 
6. rx stat should also tell if there is anything wrong.
