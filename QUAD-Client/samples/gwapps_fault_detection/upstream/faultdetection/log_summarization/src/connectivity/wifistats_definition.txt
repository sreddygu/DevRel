fw_tx_mgmt_subtype
    a. The stat fw_tx_mgmt_subtype represents a firmware-level counter that tracks the number of transmitted 802.11 management frames, broken down by subtype.
    b. The value at each index indicates how many frames of that subtype have been transmitted by the firmware:
        0  - association_request        : Sent by a client to request association
        1  - association_response       : Sent by AP in response to association request
        2  - reassociation_request      : Sent by client to reassociate with AP
        3  - reassociation_response     : Sent by AP in response to reassociation request
        4  - probe_request              : Sent by client to discover APs
        5  - probe_response             : Sent by AP in response to probe request
        6  - timing_advertisement       : Sent by AP to advertise timing information
        7  - reserved                   : Not used
        8  - beacon                     : Periodic broadcast by AP
        9  - atim                       : Used in ad hoc networks
        10 - disassociation             : Sent to terminate association
        11 - authentication             : Sent to initiate authentication
        12 - deauthentication           : Sent to terminate authentication
        13 - action                     : Used for various control actions
        14 - action_no_ack              : Action frame that does not require ACK
        15 - reserved                   : Not used
fw_rx_mgmt_subtype
    a. The stat fw_rx_mgmt_subtype represents a firmware-level counter that tracks the number of received 802.11 management frames, broken down by subtype. 
    b. Each index corresponds to a specific frame subtype as defined by the IEEE 802.11 standard:
        0  - association_request        : Received from a client requesting association
        1  - association_response       : Received from AP in response to association request
        2  - reassociation_request      : Received from client attempting to reassociate
        3  - reassociation_response     : Received from AP in response to reassociation request
        4  - probe_request              : Received from client scanning for APs
        5  - probe_response             : Received from AP responding to probe request
        6  - timing_advertisement       : Received from AP advertising timing information
        7  - reserved                   : Not used
        8  - beacon                     : Received periodically from APs
        9  - atim                       : Received in ad hoc networks
        10 - disassociation             : Received to terminate association
        11 - authentication             : Received to initiate authentication
        12 - deauthentication           : Received to terminate authentication
        13 - action                     : Received for various control actions
        14 - action_no_ack              : Received action frame that does not require ACK
        15 - reserved                   : Not used
