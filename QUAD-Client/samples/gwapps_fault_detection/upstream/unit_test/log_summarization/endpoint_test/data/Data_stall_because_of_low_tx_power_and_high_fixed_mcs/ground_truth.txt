4. Mitigation / Next Steps

Immediate Low-Risk Mitigations
1. Adjust CCA Threshold (if applicable):

2. Check and Optimize TX Power:
iwconfig ath1 | grep "Tx-Power"

3. Enable Rate Control Debugging:
cfg80211tool ath1 dbgLVL 0x80c00000
Verification Steps
1. Capture Enhanced Statistics:
# Beacon statistics

# Detailed TX statistics

2. Monitor Real-time Metrics:
# Check for RX stuck conditions (6GHz specific)
