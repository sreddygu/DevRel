Observed Symptom

2. Root Cause Analysis

Primary Suspected Causes (Ranked by Likelihood)
HIGH Likelihood: AP-initiated disconnect due to STA's deauth attempt
• The AP detected a deauth frame from the STA (fc:b3:bc:f0:b0:c2) and initiated a disconnect by issuing a DEL_STATION command before receiving the STA's deauth frame. This indicates that the AP took the initiative to disconnect the STA, likely due to the STA's attempt to deauthenticate.
• Evidence: - RX DEAUTH frame from STA
- AP issued DEL_STATION before receiving deauth from STA
