MEDIUM Likelihood: Delayed Beacon Tx causing authentication failures
• The "SAE: Confirm mismatch" error message indicates that the WPA3-SAE authentication failed due to a password mismatch. However, the timing of the error message suggests that there may be underlying link management issues causing the authentication failures. The extensive MLO activity in the event sequence indicates that the AP is experiencing link management issues, which can be linked to delayed beacon transmissions. When the AP fails to transmit beacons on time, STAs may not be able to synchronize with the AP, leading to authentication failures.

- The event sequence shows multiple attempts to establish a connection between the STA and the AP, with several disconnections and timing anomalies.
- Delayed Beacon Tx is a common cause of link management issues.
