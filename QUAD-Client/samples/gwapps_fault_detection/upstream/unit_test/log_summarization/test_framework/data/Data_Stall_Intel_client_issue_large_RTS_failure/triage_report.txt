Observed Symptom

2. Root Cause Analysis

Primary Suspected Causes (Ranked by Likelihood)
MEDIUM Likelihood: Low RTS success rate causing data stall
• The RTS success rate measures the percentage of successful RTS transmissions. A low RTS success rate indicates that the AP is unable to obtain CTS responses, which is necessary for the AP to transmit data frames. The high failure rate in the RTS success rate indicates that the AP is experiencing significant channel access impairment, leading to data stalls.
• Evidence: Stats show rts_success_rate = 13.8% on mac_id 0 (86.2% failure rate) [severity: HIGH]. This indicates the AP cannot obtain CTS responses, severely impairing channel access and causing the data stall.
MEDIUM Likelihood: CCA OBSS channel congestion
• The CCA OBSS busy time indicates the percentage of time the channel is occupied by OBSS traffic. A high CCA OBSS busy time can cause channel access failures, leading to data stalls. However, the low severity of the CCA OBSS congestion (7.4%) suggests that it is not the primary cause of the data stall.
• Evidence: CCA OBSS busy time on channel 6295: 7.4%
